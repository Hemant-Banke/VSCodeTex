# Writing helpers

- **Paste a screenshot** into a `.tex` file: it's saved under `figures/` and inserted as a figure with a caption and label. Choose *Convert image to LaTeX with AI* from the paste menu to turn a picture of an equation or table into LaTeX instead.
- **Drag images or `.tex` files** from the Explorer into the editor for `\includegraphics` or `\input`.
- **Add Citation** (⌃L ⇧C / Alt+L Shift+C): paste a DOI or arXiv link, or pick from Zotero (with Better BibTeX). The entry goes into your `.bib` file and `\cite{…}` goes at the cursor.
- **Suggestions** for `\cite` keys, `\ref` labels with their numbers, commands, environments and file paths, and **hover previews** of math, citations and figures.
